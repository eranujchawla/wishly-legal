OnCue — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-03

OnCue helps you remember birthdays, anniversaries and other dates for the people you care about, and gets a message ready for you to send. This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is kept and what control you have. OnCue is published by DÆRA ("we", "us"). We have written it to be true of the version of the app you are using. It takes effect on 27 September 2026.

The short version

Everything you add to OnCue is stored on your phone. You sign in with your Google account to use OnCue; Google tells OnCue your name, email address and an account identifier, and these are kept on your phone too. This version of OnCue has no server: we do not receive, see or store your information.

OnCue contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting services, and it does not use artificial intelligence (AI) to process your information.

A message leaves your phone only when you choose to send it: OnCue opens WhatsApp or your messaging app with the message filled in, and you press Send there. OnCue never sends a message by itself.

What OnCue stores, on your phone

About the people you add: their name, nickname, relationship to you and how they are linked to others in your family, phone number, email address, postal address, photo, notes you write, whether they are a favourite, the tones you allow for them, and dates such as birthdays and anniversaries, with the year if you know it.

The messages OnCue suggests and any you write or edit, with the last five wordings of each so you can go back to one, the time you chose for each, which occasions you have opened a message for, and whether you told OnCue it was sent.

About your account: the Google account you signed in with (its email address and Google’s identifier for it), when you signed in, and whether you chose to stay signed in.

About you: the name you choose to enter, your own photo, gender and mobile number if you add them, your own birthday if you add it, your usual tone, your time zone (detected from your phone unless you choose one), and whether and when you want the evening summary.

All of this is kept in the app’s private storage on your device. It is not copied to us or to anyone else.

Where the information comes from

From you, when you type it in.

From Google, when you sign in: your name, email address and Google’s identifier for your account. OnCue asks Google for nothing else — not your contacts, calendar, gender or phone number; you add those yourself if you wish.

From your contacts, only when you ask. "Choose from contacts" copies the details of the one contact you pick. "Import from contacts" shows your contacts so you can choose several, and copies only the ones you select: name, phone numbers, birthday and other dates, nickname, email, postal address and photo. Your contact list itself is never uploaded, and contacts you do not choose are not saved.

From your photo library, only for a photo you choose. The system photo picker gives OnCue only the picture you tap. Every photo is shrunk to a small square before it is saved.

Permissions

Contacts (Android: READ_CONTACTS). Asked for only after OnCue has explained why, when you choose or import contacts. You can refuse, and you can still add people by typing their details. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone’s settings.

Photos. OnCue uses the system photo picker, which needs no photo-library permission on current Android versions. OnCue does not request storage access on any Android version. OnCue does not use your camera or microphone.

Notifications (Android 13 and later: POST_NOTIFICATIONS). Asked for once, when you first open OnCue, for the evening summary. You can refuse; everything else works the same. OnCue also uses RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED so its scheduled notifications still arrive after your phone restarts.

Internet. Used to sign in with Google and for the optional address search described below.

Notifications

The evening before a day with birthdays, anniversaries or other occasions, around the time you choose (8 pm unless you change it), OnCue shows one notification that names them. It is scheduled and shown on your phone by the phone’s own notification system. Its content never leaves your phone: it is not sent through any push service, to us or to anyone else.

Each occasion can also have its own reminder, on the day or up to 30 days before at a time you choose, or none; you set it when you add or edit the date. It is scheduled and shown on your phone in the same way.

When you approve a message and choose a time, OnCue also shows a notification at the time you chose on the day, naming the person. Tapping it opens WhatsApp or your messaging app with the message filled in, and you press Send; OnCue never sends it for you. Un-approving or sending the message removes the reminder.

Because they name people, anyone who can see your screen may see them. Turn the evening summary off or change its time in Settings → Notifications, or turn off OnCue’s notifications in your phone’s settings.

What leaves your phone

Messages you send. When you tap WhatsApp or Text, OnCue opens that app with the recipient and message filled in. What happens next — whether you send it, and how it is delivered — is between you and that app, under its own terms and privacy policy. If WhatsApp cannot be opened, OnCue says so and opens nothing else — it does not fall back to WhatsApp’s website. OnCue is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

Invitations. When you tap Invite, OnCue opens WhatsApp, your messaging app or your phone’s share menu with an invitation filled in. It contains only a link to OnCue on Google Play — nothing about you or the person you invite — and you decide whether to send it.

Address search, where available. In versions of the app where address search is switched on, the address text you type is sent to Geoapify, our address-lookup provider, to suggest matching addresses, together with your country (taken from your phone’s language settings) to rank the results. Like any website, Geoapify also receives your device’s internet (IP) address. No name, phone number or other detail about the person goes with it. Where address search is not available, you type the address in and nothing is sent.

Nothing else. OnCue does not sell, rent or share your information, and does not send it to us.

Third-party services and components

OnCue is built with open-source software (React Native and Expo libraries) that runs on your phone and sends nothing to the people who make it. OnCue contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software.

Google Play distributes OnCue. Downloading and updating it is covered by Google’s own privacy policy; OnCue does not receive that information.

Google Sign-In handles signing in, through your phone’s Google services and under Google’s own privacy policy. Google knows that you signed in to OnCue; OnCue receives only your name, email address and account identifier, and keeps them on your phone. You can remove OnCue’s access in your Google Account, under Security → Your connections to third-party apps.

WhatsApp (Meta) and your phone’s messaging app receive a message, and the number it is for, only when you tap WhatsApp or Text and then send it — under their own privacy policies.

Geoapify receives the address text you type, your country and your device’s internet address, only in versions where address search is switched on (see above).

How long information is kept

Everything you add is kept on your phone until you delete it, delete everything, or uninstall OnCue. We keep nothing, because we receive nothing. Geoapify keeps address searches as its own policy describes.

Information about other people

OnCue stores details about people in your life, including children if you add them. Please add only information you have a reasonable right to keep and use to stay in touch with them.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your phone until you delete it.

You can change or remove details at any time in the app — for example an address, a photo, a link between people, or every saved phone number at once (Settings → Your data → Remove all phone numbers).

Settings → Your data → Delete everything permanently removes everyone and everything you have added, including your own details and any photo copies the app cached. It cannot be undone, because there is no copy anywhere else.

Uninstalling OnCue, or clearing its storage in your phone’s settings, also deletes all of it.

Security

Your information is kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read, and it is excluded from your phone’s cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer when you set up a new phone. Because it is not transmitted to us, there is no copy on a server to be exposed. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the app, so please protect your phone with a lock.

Children

OnCue is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is not designed for or directed at children.

Your rights

Under Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) you have the right to know what personal information is held about you, to have it corrected and to withdraw your consent. Because OnCue keeps your information only on your phone, you can see, correct and delete all of it yourself in the app.

DÆRA is responsible for this policy and for how OnCue handles personal information. If you have a question, a request or a concern about privacy, write to our privacy contact at the address below; we will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca).

If you live in the European Union, the United Kingdom or California

EU and UK (GDPR and UK GDPR). OnCue processes the information you add only on your phone, at your direction; we do not receive it. You can access, correct, export by viewing, restrict and erase all of it yourself in the app. For any question, or to complain, contact us; you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA). We do not sell or share personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we collect none from the app.

Children (COPPA and similar laws). OnCue is not directed at children under 13, or at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Information about a child that an adult user adds stays on that user’s phone.

Features that are not switched on

We may later offer optional backup of your information, or messages written with the help of AI. Neither is part of this version. Before either is switched on we will update this policy, tell you in the app what would change, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for any significant change, tell you in the app. The current version is always available in the app under Settings → Privacy policy and at eranujchawla.github.io/oncue-legal.
